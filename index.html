Official Guidelines

Ten-Article Tournament Policy Proposal

Review the complete ten-article policy proposal before joining a team or submitting registration payment.

ARTICLE I — Tournament Overview +

Titan Crown Racing hosts a weekly international horse racing team tournament promoting teamwork, competitive excellence, sportsmanship and international participation. The competition comprises 24 teams of 16 players each.

ARTICLE II — Registration & Participation +

24 teams · 16 registered players per team · 384 total participants · 2,000 USDT registration fee per team · 48,000 USDT projected total collections. Teams must register with the official host, provide a complete roster before the announced deadline, and receive confirmation of payment before participating.

ARTICLE III — Scoring System +

The weekly championship uses a First-Place Victory Points System. Only officially designated tournament races count toward team rankings. The host must publish the detailed points schedule before registration closes.

ARTICLE IV — Weekly Team Rankings +

The total points earned by all 16 registered players determine each team’s weekly standing. Ranks 1–4 receive championship placements, ranks 5–12 receive consolation prizes, and ranks 13–24 remain official tournament participants.

ARTICLE V — Prize Distribution +

The proposed weekly prize pool is 38,000 USDT, distributed to teams ranked 1st through 12th. The remaining 10,000 USDT of projected team fees is allocated to approved administration, operations, technical support and promotion, with transparent financial reporting. Prizes are subject to verification and applicable law.

ARTICLE VI — Tie-Breaking +

Ties are resolved by (1) number of first-place finishes, (2) number of second-place finishes, (3) number of third-place finishes, and (4) an officially supervised tie-breaker race under conditions announced in advance.

ARTICLE VII — Responsibilities & Fair Play +

Race manipulation, false results, unauthorized roster changes, collusion, fraud, interference, unauthorized software and exploits are prohibited. Investigations may result in point deductions, prize forfeiture or disqualification.

ARTICLE VIII — Results & Prize Distribution +

The host maintains verified weekly standings, first-place victories and team totals. Final results follow verification. Payment deadlines, wallets, deductions and protest periods must be disclosed before registration.

ARTICLE IX — Management & Authority +

Titan Crown Racing manages registration, roster verification, scheduling, results, rankings, prize accounting, dispute resolution and policy announcements. Operations involving fees or monetary prizes are subject to applicable laws, gaming rules, licenses and platform requirements; no event begins before required authorizations.

ARTICLE X — Official Declaration +

24 ELITE TEAMS. 384 COMPETITIVE PLAYERS. ONE WEEKLY CHAMPION. Every victory counts. Every point matters. Every team has an opportunity to rise to the top. Official policy proposal subject to review, regulatory compliance and final management approval.
